TD STUDY CENTER — Privacy Policy Last update: November 2026 TD STUDY CENTER ("App") provides mock tests, PDF books, video classes and notes for the preparation of West Bengal Police, SSC, PSC and other government job exams. How your information is used is explained below. 1. What information do we collect? Account information: Email address and password (passwords are stored securely in Firebase Authentication, we don't see them). Test information: History of your test answers, scores, and results. Rank List: After the mock test, your name (name given during signup), number, and time can be seen by other users of the app in the rank list of the app. Email is not shown. TOOLS section: Notes, reading time calculators, and reading plans stay on your phone only, not on our servers. PDF/image tool files are created on the phone, not uploaded anywhere. Payment information: Name of item purchased, amount, Transaction ID (UTR) or Razorpay payment ID, and date of purchase. We do not collect or store your card, UPI PIN, or bank account information. Device and usage information: Advertising ID for ads and notifications, device type, and general app usage information. 2. Why do we use information? To login and operate your account, open your purchased items, and maintain the validity (1 year from the date of payment). To show test results and history. To verify payments and prevent fraud. To show ads (the app is ad-supported) and send notifications about new tests or updates. 3. Third-party services The app uses the following services. They have their own privacy policies: Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy Google AdMob (Advertising) — policies.google.com/privacy OneSignal (Notifications) — onesignal.com/privacy_policy Razorpay (online payments, if enabled) — razorpay.com/privacy We do not sell your personal information. 4. Data protection and security Information is stored on Google Firebase's secure servers over HTTPS. Your information is only kept as long as your account is active. 5. Deleting accounts and information From within the app: Tap the logout icon in the top right of the home screen → "Permanently delete account and data" → Confirm with your password. This will delete your account and test results. Without the app: Send "Delete Account" from your registered email to the email below. We will delete it within a reasonable time. Payment records (item, amount, date, Transaction ID) may be kept separately for accounting and legal purposes. 6. Children's information The app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect their information. 7. Changes in policy If this policy changes, it will be announced on this page along with the new date. 8.
